# No corporate jargon

Customer-facing English prose uses concrete words instead of corporate jargon.

Markdown and plain-text prose under src/ avoids “synergy”, “game-changing”,
“cutting-edge”, and “leverage”. Suitable alternatives are “cooperation”, a
specific description of the improvement, “new”, and “use”. Matching is
case-insensitive and applies to whole words or the complete hyphenated phrase.

Source code, internal notes outside src/, and unrelated words such as
“leveraged” are outside this rule's scope.
